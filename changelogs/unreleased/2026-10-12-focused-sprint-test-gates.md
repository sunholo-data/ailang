### Changed — sprint executors run focused tests, not the full suite (2026-10-12)

New `make test-changed`: tests the Go packages the branch touches (committed, uncommitted and untracked, vs the merge-base with `origin/dev`; override with `TEST_CHANGED_BASE`) plus `make test-core`. The sprint-executor skill and its scripts (session start, prerequisites, milestone checkpoint) now use it instead of the full `make test`, and the skill no longer runs the full suite when it loads. The sprint-planner checklist now requires focused gates, changelog fragments, companion files near the 800-line limit, and milestones sized for one executor run. The full suite stays in CI. On the 8 GiB cloud executor with a RAM-backed `/tmp`, full runs took hours, hit SIGBUS, and were a leading cause of 3h timeouts that lost all work.
