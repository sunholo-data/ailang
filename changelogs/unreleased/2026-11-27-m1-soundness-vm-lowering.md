### Fixed — Cross-module record `Eq` soundness, bytecode VM trap isolation, exhaustive `CoreExpr` lowering, and strict JSON boundary decoding (2026-11-27)

- **Nominal record `Eq` & VM closure equality trap (`SOUND-1`)**: Added `TypeModule` to `types.TRecord` (preserved across unification, substitution, alias closure, `iface` JSON, and the `CoreTypeInfo` DAG cache, bumping cache schema to `v15`) and keyed `InstanceEnv.eqRecordNames` by `NominalKey(Module, Name)` via `HasDerivedEqRecord`, preventing cross-module record aliases from hijacking an imported type's `deriving (Eq)`. Bytecode VM `OpEq`/`OpNeq` and list equality builtins now trap with `RT_EQ_001` on direct or container-nested closures while preserving `bytecode.Value.Equal` for constant-pool deduplication. Added an open-record pattern hint (`{a, b, ...}`) on contravariant record field mismatches.
- **No evaluator replay after bytecode VM dispatch (`#506`)**: `tryRunEntryViaVM` now treats any post-dispatch `machine.Run` error (`RT001`, stack overflow, `RT_EQ_001`) as final without falling back to the tree-walking evaluator or doubling the `"vm: "` error prefix, while keeping pre-dispatch `BC001`/bridge-ineligible fallback intact under `--bytecode`.
- **Exhaustive `CoreExpr` traversal in `OpLowerer` and `Linker` (`#2323`)**: Lowered operators inside `Tuple`, `Array`, `RecordUpdate`, `DictAbs`, `DictApp`, and `Forall` in `OpLowerer`, and extended `Linker` dictionary-reference collection to walk `prog.Meta[*].Contracts[*].Expr`, `Match` guards, `Tuple`, `Array`, `RecordUpdate`, `Intrinsic`, and `Forall`.
- **Strict `--args-json` `int` decoding & `serve-api` `std/json.Json` binding (`#2399`)**: Switched `argdecode` to `json.NewDecoder` with `UseNumber()` to preserve 64-bit integer precision and reject fractional or out-of-range floats for `int` parameters, and added `std/json.Json` `ParamSchema` recognition and recursive `TaggedValue` (`JNull`, `JBool`, `JInt`, `JNumber`, `JString`, `JArray`, `JObject`) binding in `internal/apiserver`.
