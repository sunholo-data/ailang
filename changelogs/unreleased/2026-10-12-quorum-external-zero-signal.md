### Fixed — design-quorum zero-signal guard counts present external reviewers only (2026-10-12)

A design-quorum run whose external reviewers were all absent could still `proceed` when the in-session controller passed, because the controller was counted as a present participant before the zero-signal guard. The guard now counts present external reviewer outcomes only. A controller-only "quorum" blocks with the existing zero-signal objection, while the controller keeps its veto and its artifact entry. This is an intentional behavior change: controller-only runs move from `proceed` to `blocked` (exit 3 at the CLI). Tracked as fleet ticket `quorum:zero-signal-guard-vacuous-with-controller-verdict`, reported at [ailang#651](https://github.com/sunholo-data/ailang/issues/651).
