### Fixed

- **M-WORKER-FOLLOWUP: acknowledged policy-worker cleanup.** Owned managed and async children invoking the same runtime now propagate cancellation through a private lifecycle channel. Successful cancellation joins separately grouped policy workers, direct-child reaping and owned IO under the existing shared deadline; missing acknowledgements fail visibly. Policy admission, confinement, timeout and output caps retain their boundaries.
- **Keyless AI CLI help and offline mode.** `ailang run` freezes provider configuration at startup and creates the provider on the first authorized AI effect. Generated AI-enabled `[bin]` entries can handle application `--help` and `--offline` without keys or provider readiness checks. Real AI use retains authentication failures, optional image/speech/stream interfaces and budget checks; explicit eager service setup and stub validation remain available.

Design: [M-WORKER-FOLLOWUP](../design_docs/implemented/v0_54_5/m-worker-policy-and-lazy-ai.md). Runnable examples: `ai_offline_cli.ail` and `process_policy_cancel.ail` with its restricted companion fixture. The same-executable lifecycle boundary requires the upgraded runtime on both sides; stopping a local worker does not promise remote inference or billing cancellation.
